# AI-assisted admin policy draft

Reviewed: 7 July 2026.

Purpose: set safe boundaries before Healing Hands Network uses AI-assisted
tools for administration, drafting or reporting.

This is a draft for discussion. It should be reviewed by the charity before any
AI-assisted process handles charity information.

## Policy position

AI tools may help with low-risk drafting and organisation. They must not make
decisions for the charity, replace safeguarding judgement, provide therapy or
medical advice, or process sensitive personal information without a separate
data-protection decision.

## Allowed low-risk uses

AI may be used to help:

- turn approved notes into first-draft website copy;
- summarise non-sensitive meeting notes;
- draft fundraising or thank-you wording for human review;
- reorganise public information into clearer formats;
- prepare checklists, templates and project plans;
- suggest plain-English rewrites of already-approved content.

## Not allowed without separate approval

Do not use AI to:

- assess beneficiaries, trauma, PTSD, treatment needs or safeguarding risk;
- provide medical, therapy, legal, crisis or immigration advice;
- decide who receives support, funding, treatment or priority;
- process names, contact details, health information, stories or photographs
  of beneficiaries or volunteers;
- draft public claims, impact figures or quotes without evidence and approval;
- upload private emails, passwords, bank details, donor records or volunteer
  records;
- generate images of real people or events in a way that could mislead.

## Human review requirements

Anything AI helps draft must be checked by a person before use.

For public website or social content, the reviewer should confirm:

- factual accuracy;
- tone and dignity;
- consent for any stories, names, quotes or images;
- safeguarding/privacy risk;
- whether the wording overclaims impact;
- whether the charity is comfortable being publicly associated with it.

## Data rules

- Use public or already-approved information wherever possible.
- Remove names, contact details and identifying details before using AI.
- Do not paste personal stories into AI tools unless explicit permission and a
  data-protection basis have been agreed.
- Do not treat AI output as a source of truth.
- Keep private evidence and approval records outside the public website
  repository.

## Website-specific workflow

1. Collect update from Susan, trustee, volunteer or partner.
2. Remove unnecessary personal or sensitive details.
3. Use AI only for structure or wording if appropriate.
4. Check against evidence and consent.
5. Add as draft website content.
6. Review on preview.
7. Publish only after charity approval.

## Red lines for this charity

Because Healing Hands Network works with people affected by war, trauma,
veterans and humanitarian need, the following areas require extra caution:

- therapy outcomes;
- PTSD or trauma language;
- images or stories of vulnerable people;
- locations that could expose people or partners;
- war-related claims that may become outdated quickly;
- donor or beneficiary personal data.

## First safe use cases

The safest first uses are internal and non-sensitive:

- converting approved update notes into a draft news item;
- summarising the website review feedback;
- preparing grant-application skeletons from approved charity boilerplate;
- drafting social posts from already-approved website updates.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Charity agrees whether AI tools may be used at all.
- [ ] A named person is responsible for AI-assisted workflows.
- [ ] Sensitive-data rules are understood.
- [ ] Public-content review process is agreed.
- [ ] Safeguarding/privacy escalation route is agreed.
- [ ] This policy is reviewed before any real beneficiary, volunteer or donor
      information is processed.
