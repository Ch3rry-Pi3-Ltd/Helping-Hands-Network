# Susan review pack

Reviewed: 7 July 2026.

Prototype link:
[https://helping-hands-network.vercel.app/](https://helping-hands-network.vercel.app/)

This pack is written for Susan and the Healing Hands Network trustees. It
explains what has been prepared, what is still draft and what feedback would
be most useful.

## What has been done so far

- A separate first-draft website prototype has been built.
- The current public WordPress site has not been changed.
- The prototype has clearer pages for the charity's mission, work, volunteering,
  support/donations, updates and contact.
- The site has been tested for mobile layout, accessibility, broken internal
  links, metadata, sitemap, security headers and performance.
- Privacy-friendly aggregate analytics have been added so broad visitor trends
  can be seen without trying to identify individual visitors.
- A practical roadmap has been started for the wider proposal ideas, including
  impact reporting, donation-route clarity and future content updates.

## Important caveat

This is still a prototype. It is not being treated as the final public charity
website until the charity has reviewed and approved the content.

Some pages deliberately include notes where details need confirmation. Those
notes are there to prevent unapproved or outdated information being presented
as fact.

## Suggested review order

1. Home page — overall feel, tone and first impression.
2. Our Work — whether the three main areas are described correctly.
3. Support Us — donation routes and fundraising wording.
4. Volunteer — membership, eligibility and process.
5. Contact — public email, phone and postal details.
6. Updates — whether the proposed future news/impact structure feels useful.

## Feedback questions

### Overall impression

- Does the prototype feel like Healing Hands Network?
- Is the tone warm, respectful and accurate?
- Does anything feel too corporate, too informal or off-brand?

### Content accuracy

- Which sections are factually wrong or out of date?
- Which programme details need changing?
- Are any important areas of work missing?
- Are there any statements that should be softened or removed?

### Donation and support routes

- Which donation link should be the main call to action?
- Are the current JustGiving, GoFundMe, GivingLottery, standing-order and Gift
  Aid routes still correct?
- Who should receive fundraising or sponsorship enquiries?

### Images and stories

- Are the current logo and photographs acceptable to use?
- Do any images need removing or replacing?
- Are captions or alternative text needed for specific photographs?
- Are any quotes or stories approved for public use?

### Updates and impact

- Would a simple Updates/Impact page be useful?
- What kind of updates would be easiest for the charity to provide?
- Who should approve impact summaries before publication?

## Information needed before launch

- Confirmed public email address.
- Confirmed public phone number.
- Confirmed postal address.
- Approved primary donation route.
- Approved programme descriptions.
- Approved volunteer information.
- Image permissions and preferred captions.
- Final approval to connect the real domain.
