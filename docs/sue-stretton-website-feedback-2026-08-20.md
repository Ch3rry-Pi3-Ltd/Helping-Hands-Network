## Summary

### Overall response

Sue is very positive about the refreshed design and approach and is keen for the new website to launch. Her feedback consists mainly of factual corrections, updated programme information, preferred fundraising routes and additional content that could be incorporated.

### Immediate corrections

- Capitalise **Siege** in the recognised title **Siege of Sarajevo**.
- Present Healing Hands Network as having **two principal areas of work**, rather than three, because it has undertaken little or no direct UK veterans work since Covid.
- Do not present JustGiving as the charity's main fundraising platform. Sue prefers **GoFundMe**, which is less costly to the charity, while the charity remains registered with JustGiving.

### Fundraising and donation information

- Include **The Giving Lottery** and **Give as You Live** as fundraising routes once Sue supplies the correct links and approved promotional material.
- Consider presenting suggested sponsored activities such as walking, running, jumping, firewalking, climbing, sponsored haircuts and giving something up for a month.
- Consider community fundraising suggestions including coffee mornings, craft workshops, cake or craft sales, garden parties, Halloween events, collections and book sales.
- Note that supporters may donate by bank transfer and request a Gift Aid form, but public instructions and any forms should be confirmed before publication.
- The charity has an Amazon wishlist, although Sue needs to check that the items and prices remain current before it is promoted.

### Bosnia and Herzegovina programme

- Partner associations refer their members most in need at the beginning of each season: the Concentration Camp Union, Civil War Victims, Women Victims of War and Mothers of Srebrenica.
- The usual Sarajevo season runs from **1 May to 31 July**, with the possibility of extension when enough volunteers are available.
- Volunteers may attend for one week, two weeks—the usual and preferred duration—or longer.
- Volunteer contributions cover travel, accommodation, basic food, clinic and outreach running costs, translators, local management, airport transfers and transport to outreach venues. Personal purchases, optional meals out and weekend sightseeing are additional.

### Current UK activity

- The charity currently supports the military FABCAMPS residential weeks in Wales and Scotland by providing free therapies to bereaved military families.
- This is presently its only active UK work.
- Further work through veteran support centres, monthly groups or veterans' cafés may be possible, but local volunteer coverage is not currently in place.

### Ukraine activity

- A trip delivering surgical, medical and humanitarian aid, generators and related supplies costs approximately **£1,600**.
- This figure should be treated as an approximate current cost and reconfirmed before being used in prominent fundraising claims.

### Membership and volunteering

- The charity needs more members and volunteers for its Bosnia work.
- Professionally qualified and insured therapists practising mainstream modalities may join as travelling volunteers or supporter members.
- Therapists who cannot travel can still support and promote the charity.
- Non-therapists may also join as supporters.
