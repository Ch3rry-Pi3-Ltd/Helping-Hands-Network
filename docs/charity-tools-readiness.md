# Charity tools readiness checklist

Reviewed: 7 July 2026.

Purpose: prepare a low-risk review of useful charity technology programmes
before Susan or the trustees decide whether to apply.

This document does not create accounts, submit applications or change existing
systems. It records what to check, what access is needed and what the charity
would need to own afterwards.

| Charity Digital / TechSoup UK | Discounted or donated software options. | Verify current UK access route, eligibility and admin fees. | Are any products actually needed and maintainable? |

## Minimum account-governance rules

- Use a charity-owned email account where possible, not a personal account.
- Record the account owner, backup owner and recovery method privately.
- Enable multi-factor authentication where supported.
- Do not share passwords by email or in the website repository.
- Avoid creating tools unless someone can maintain them after launch.
- Record renewal dates, fees and cancellation routes.

## Google Search Console checklist

Complete after the final domain is approved and connected.

- [ ] Decide the Google account that will own the property.
- [ ] Add the final domain property.
- [ ] Verify domain ownership using the agreed DNS method.
- [ ] Submit `https://<final-domain>/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Check indexing status after launch.
- [ ] Review search queries after several weeks.
- [ ] Add Susan/trustee/technical maintainer access if appropriate.
- [ ] Record who receives issue alerts.

## Google for Nonprofits checklist

Google states that an organisation generally needs to be a nonprofit charitable
organisation in good standing, verified through its validation partner, and not
fall into excluded categories such as governmental entities, hospitals/healthcare
organisations or schools.

- [ ] Confirm Healing Hands Network's registration information.
- [ ] Confirm whether the charity is eligible under Google's UK requirements.
- [ ] Choose the Google account owner.
- [ ] Prepare charity registration evidence.
- [ ] Confirm who can verify affiliation with the charity.
- [ ] Decide which products are actually wanted before applying.
- [ ] Record account recovery and ongoing maintenance responsibilities.

## Google Ad Grants readiness

Google describes Ad Grants as in-kind advertising on Google Search, with a
monthly advertising value for text ads. This is only useful if the charity can
maintain clear campaigns and review performance.

- [ ] Confirm Google for Nonprofits eligibility first.
- [ ] Confirm the final website domain is live, accurate and approved.
- [ ] Decide campaign goals: donations, volunteers, awareness or event support.
- [ ] Identify landing pages for each goal.
- [ ] Decide who will monitor search terms, ads and policy compliance.
- [ ] Avoid campaigns around sensitive therapy, trauma or crisis terms unless
      wording has been carefully reviewed.
- [ ] Use aggregate measurement only unless a separate privacy review approves
      more detailed tracking.

## Canva Nonprofits readiness

Canva says eligible charitable organisations can receive access to premium
tools, subject to review and country-specific verification.

- [ ] Confirm eligibility and required documents.
- [ ] Decide who owns the Canva team.
- [ ] Create or collect approved logo files, colours and fonts.
- [ ] Create templates only after Susan approves the website direction.
- [ ] Record who can publish public graphics.
- [ ] Do not upload sensitive beneficiary or volunteer images unless consent is
      recorded.

## Charity Digital / TechSoup readiness

Treat this as a software-procurement route, not a general recommendation to add
tools.

- [ ] Recheck the current UK route and eligibility.
- [ ] List the actual software need before browsing offers.
- [ ] Confirm any admin fee, renewal fee or licence restriction.
- [ ] Confirm who owns the licence and account.
- [ ] Avoid duplicate tools that add login or data-protection overhead.

## Recommended order

1. Search Console after final domain approval.
2. Canva Nonprofits if Susan wants easier branded design templates.
3. Google for Nonprofits only if there is a clear owner.
4. Ad Grants only after the site content and donation/volunteer journeys are
   approved.
5. Charity Digital / TechSoup only when there is a specific software need.
